An independent educational initiative based on my proprietary technical analysis models, empirical market studies, and systematic trading methodologies.

Students learn to observe and interpret the dynamics of market movements through proprietary analytical models, examining impulses, expansions, exhaustion phases, and changing market conditions rather than relying exclusively on conventional price patterns or directional predictions.

## 🔬 EFM (End Flow Momentum) — Strategic Investment Opportunity

**EFM (End Flow Momentum)** is a proprietary quantitative trading framework developed through more than seven years of independent empirical research.

Implemented as an MQL5 Expert Advisor for MetaTrader 5, EFM combines statistical market-regime detection with an adaptive capital-exposure mechanism.

Its distinguishing feature is a self-referential risk-management architecture that identifies structural changes in the strategy's own account-balance series rather than relying exclusively on underlying asset prices.

### Quantitative Validation

EFM has undergone statistical evaluation using historical XAUUSD (M5) simulations, including:

- Probabilistic Sharpe Ratio (PSR)
- Structural break detection
- Walk-forward analysis
- Monte Carlo block-bootstrap simulations
- Dynamic robust risk measures

The research identified statistically significant differences in movement magnitude and dispersion under certain market regimes.

**Directional predictability was not statistically established**, and the dynamic position-sizing mechanism remains a significant source of tail risk.

## 📊 Research Frameworks

### 🔹 Finite Lifetime Convexity Optimization (FLCO)

A quantitative capital allocation framework designed for individuals operating under finite and uncertain human lifetime constraints.

Traditional portfolio optimization models implicitly assume long or indefinite horizons and prioritize volatility-adjusted performance metrics.

FLCO reformulates the objective function by explicitly incorporating lifetime uncertainty as a stochastic constraint in the decision process.

Instead of maximizing return per unit of variance, FLCO defines the optimization objective as:

**Maximize:**

\[
P(\tau_{\pi} \leq T)
\]

Where:

- `τ_π` = stopping time required for strategy `π` to reach a predefined wealth target.
- `T` = remaining lifetime modeled as a random variable.

Under this framework, strategy evaluation emphasizes:

- Positive convexity and asymmetric payoff structures
- Geometric growth rate (GHPR)
- Probability of ruin and capital survivability
- Inter-arrival time of extreme gains
- Cross-regime robustness

FLCO integrates stochastic growth theory, convex payoff dynamics, and mortality-adjusted decision modeling into a unified framework for accelerated wealth transformation.

📌 **Research status:** Whitepaper, mathematical formalization, and simulations in development.
